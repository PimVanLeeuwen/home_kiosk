Lights example data

The living room through the Hue Bridge: the kitchen spots and the ceiling lamps as two tiles that glow in the white they have now. Tap a tile to switch it, drag the bar for brightness, pick a white for the spots. Click around: the tiles respond.

The page

  • Under a new Home group in the menu. Only the living room (decided 2026-10-10); the other rooms are not shown.
  • Kitchen spots (Keuken 1, 2, 3, Hue ambiance): on or off, brightness, and five whites from warm 2200 K to cool 6500 K. Ceiling lamps (Plafondlamp Bank and Tafel, filament bulbs): on or off and brightness.
  • The tile glows in the white the lights have now, stronger when brighter; dark when off. Below, each light as a chip, so one spot that is off or unreachable shows.
  • "All off" for the room. No scenes (dropped 2026-10-10).

Live

  • The hub listens to the bridge's event stream, so a change from the switch, Siri or the Home app shows within two seconds. A change here is sent at once and the tile follows what the bridge confirms.
  • A light the bridge cannot reach is shown as unreachable on its chip. If the bridge itself is unreachable, the tiles are dimmed with "Last known state" and the controls are off.
