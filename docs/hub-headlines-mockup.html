Headlines on News example data

The Now panel's Headlines screen in the hub, at the top of News: the newest story of each group, the one with the best image as the lead over its picture, the other two beside it with thumbnails; tap a story to read it, the bookmark saves it to Readeck and fills.

Which stories

  • One per group: the newest story of Tech, Nederland and Self-hosted, Apple & Linux, so it is always a mix.
  • The lead is the newest of the three that has a big enough image; the others follow, newest first.
  • Refreshed with the feeds; a story you have opened stays until a newer one comes in.

Images

  • From the feed (media:content, media:thumbnail, an image enclosure); for a headline without one, the article page's og:image, looked up once.
  • Fetched, resized and cached by the hub (as the Media posters), never loaded from the news site by the browser. A story without an image gets a tile in its group's colour with the source's initial.

A story

  • The source as a chip in its group's colour, how long ago; the lead also shows the first lines of the summary on a wide screen.
  • Tap to open it in the reader as from the list; the bookmark saves it to Readeck and fills, as Save does in the reader.
