Backups, restyled example data

The Backups page in the language of the restyled Home and NAS: last night as a seal with the route to Hetzner and 30 nights, the storage box on a dial, volumes as glass tiles, a stacked bar per run, and the dumps, restore check and mirror as checklists. Same data as today.

What changes

  • Header: as today, plus an All safe / Failed pill.
  • Last night: replaces the four summary chips. A seal with the result, time, how long it took and how much changed; the NAS to Hetzner line with its slow light (red and still after a failure); next run, success count and the restore check as three small facts; the last 30 nights as bars on the right, failed red and slow amber (as on Home).
  • Storage box: an arc gauge with the red mark at the 85% alert, used and free, the runway, and when it was measured.
  • Volumes: a glass tile each with an LED, last success, what changed, how long it took and errors; a failed volume is tinted red with rclone's last error.
  • Last 30 runs: the table stays, with an LED per run and a stacked bar of how long each volume took (a colour per volume), so a slow volume shows at a glance; failed rows are tinted. On a phone the per-volume columns give way to the bar.
  • Database dumps: a glass row per database with a pg_dump or SQLite chip, its LED, size and last success; the host job as a dot per recent run.
  • Restore check: a seal with the verdict and its steps as a checklist with ticks (a cross on a failed step, with its detail).
  • GitHub mirror: the owners as tiles with their repo counts, size, last run and next run, a dot per recent run, and failed repos in red.
