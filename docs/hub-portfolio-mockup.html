Portfolio, restyled example data

The Portfolio page in the language of the restyled Home, NAS, Backups and Docker: the value edge to edge with the invested line and the period's high, four glass facts, today's movers as a heat map (as on the Money panel), holdings with ticker chips and weight bars, allocation as a ring, and a holding's own page in the same style. Same data as today.

What changes

  • Value: the figure large with today's change as a pill; the chart runs edge to edge as a glowing area, green or red by the period's direction, with the invested line dashed and the period's high marked; the period as a segmented switch (1M, YTD, 1Y, Max) and the gain without deposits under it. The readout under the pointer stays.
  • Today, Total gain, Invested, Cash: four glass tiles under the chart, tinted figures where they move.
  • Today's movers: new on this page, the Money panel's heat map: every holding a tile sized by its weight and tinted green or red by today's move (stronger for bigger moves), the ticker, the change and, where it fits, the name. Each opens the holding. On a phone, holdings under 3% merge into one "N others" tile (it opens the holdings list), so no tile is too thin to read.
  • Holdings: ticker chips, a thin bar for the weight, today as a tinted pill with the amount, the gain tinted; the sort as a segmented switch. On a phone: chip, name, value and the sorted change.
  • Allocation: a ring instead of a bar, with the legend. Markets: each index as a glass tile with its level and tinted move.
  • A holding: the same language: the value and gain large, its price against your average price edge to edge, the figures as glass tiles, and the transactions as a timeline with a coloured chip per type.
  • Update from DEGIRO: the button becomes a glass button; the import flow itself only takes over the card styling.
