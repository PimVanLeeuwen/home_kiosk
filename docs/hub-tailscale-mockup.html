Tailscale example data

A new page under House: the tailnet as the House panel draws it (the NAS in the middle, every device around it, a running line for a direct connection, a dashed violet one through a relay), the NAS's own names and addresses beside it, and below every device as a glass tile with its MagicDNS name, addresses and how it connects. Tap a device for its details.

What the page shows

  • Header: the tailnet name and how many devices are online; a Healthy pill, or the number of health issues in amber.
  • Health: tailscaled's own health warnings (DNS, relays, the NAS logged out), each as an amber card above the map, only when there is one. Keys do not expire on this tailnet, so there is no key state.
  • Map: the same drawing as the House panel, sized to the page; on the phone it comes first, full width.
  • This NAS: host name, MagicDNS name, IPv4 and IPv6 with a copy button, the relay it calls home, and how many hub services it serves over HTTPS.
  • Devices: online first, then offline with when they were last seen. Each tile: an icon by OS glowing green, the name, the MagicDNS name, the IPv4 address, and chips for the OS, direct or which relay, and Exit node.
  • Details on tap: IPv6, the endpoint for a direct connection, traffic since tailscaled started, and the copy buttons. One tile open at a time.
  • Source: GET /localapi/v0/status on the tailscaled socket the collector already reads; read-only. When it can't be read, the last good answer with a stale note, as every page.
  • Same as the panel: names, colours and the line style, so the two read as one design.
