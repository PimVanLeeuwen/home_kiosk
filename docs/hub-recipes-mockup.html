Recipes in the hub

M30, step 0 (2026-10-27): Recipes replaces Tandoor, phone first. Prep phases (violet) always come before cook phases (amber); the line between them is where the oven or the pan goes on. Ingredients split into what to get and what to just check. JSON is the only editor: copy out to Claude, paste back in. Recipes, photos and amounts here are made up; the servings stepper and the ticks work.

01 · On the phone

Recipes takes the fourth tab (Services moves to More). The list is search, tag chips and photo cards; a recipe opens with what to get, what to check, and the phases in order.

The list

Title search, tags as filters, a "Needs review" chip for the Tandoor imports still waiting. The menu (top right) holds Paste JSON and Copy all. Prep and cook time per card, in their colours.

A recipe, top

Photo, title and the intro line, then prep and cook time and the servings stepper (try it: amounts follow). "To get" is a full list; "Check" is the pantry, small and dashed, as you only glance at it.

A recipe, the phases

Each phase a card with its own steps, the ingredients under the step that uses them. A line marks the handover from prep to heat. "Cook" stays at the bottom, in thumb reach.

02 · Cook mode

The screen stays on. One phase at a time; the bar on top shows every phase in its colour. Tap a step to tick it: it folds to one line, and the next one lights up. Ticks stay on the phone for the day.

Prepping

Large type, each step with exactly what goes in it. The steps further on are dimmed, not hidden, so you can read ahead. Tap the steps here: they tick.

All prepped

A pause before the heat: every prep phase ticked off, and the first cook step shown, so nothing starts before you are ready. One amber button to go on.

Cooking

The same layout in amber. Times are just text in the step ("25 minuten"); the oven keeps the timer.

03 · JSON in and out

No form editor. Copy a recipe (or everything) to Claude, paste the answer back. The hub checks it against the schema before anything is saved and says what changes.

Recipe menu

Copy JSON, Paste JSON (replace), a photo from the phone, and delete. The list's menu has Paste JSON (adds one or many) and Copy all.

Pasted, with errors

The JSON stays editable, the faults are marked in it and listed by path, in plain words. Save only works once it is valid.

Pasted, replacing

Same id as an existing recipe, so it replaces it after this summary. The previous version is kept once: Undo on the recipe afterwards.

Copy all

Every recipe as compact JSON, with the schema and a short note on how you cook (phases, prep first) at the top, ready to paste into a Claude chat for new ideas.

Imported from Tandoor

Each Tandoor step came in as a cook phase, every ingredient as "to get". The banner says so and copies the recipe with a prompt to rework it into phases with pantry flags.

04 · On the laptop

A new Kitchen group in the sidebar. Four cards across; a recipe keeps the photo and ingredients on the left while the phases scroll on the right.

05 · To decide

Colours for the phases

Violet for prep (the hub's accent, calm) and amber for cook (heat). Or both in violet, with only the handover line and the Cook button warm.

Recipes without a photo

Shown with the first letter on a dark tile (Koreaanse bulgogi in the list). Or leave the photo space out for those cards.

"Check" list

Small dashed chips, always open. Alternatively folded to one line ("Check 9 pantry items") until tapped.

Cook mode across phases

Swipe or the button to the next phase, with the "All prepped" pause only between the last prep phase and the first cook phase.
