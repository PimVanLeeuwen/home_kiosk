Apple devices on Home example data

The Home panel's Apple devices screen in the hub: the house as lit rooms, each Apple TV and HomePod an orb, a room glowing in colour while something plays there, light running between the Thread border routers, an offline device dashed in amber; what plays below, per room.

The house

  • Three rooms as outlined zones: the living room (Apple TV and the HomePod pair), the bedroom (Apple TV and a HomePod mini) and the bathroom (a HomePod mini). Which device is in which room is set once in config.yaml.
  • A room glows while something plays in it: blue for video, violet for music, with ripples from the device that plays.
  • Offline (not answering for a few minutes): the orb dashed in amber, the pill says how many; after 10 minutes a warning goes to Telegram.

Thread

  • The five border routers (all but the Bedroom Apple TV) are joined by faint lines with light running along them while Thread is up; a router that stops announcing Thread gets a dashed amber line. The hub sees which routers are up, not the exact mesh links, so the lines show the network, not the radio paths.
  • Below the plan: "Thread · 5 of 5 border routers".

What plays

  • One bar per room that plays: artwork (through the hub), the title, the episode or artist, the app and the room, and a moving equaliser (still while paused).
  • The HomePods report it without pairing; the living room's pair reports what its Apple TV plays. The Bedroom Apple TV is paired once with a PIN.
