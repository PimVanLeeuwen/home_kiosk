Your train on Home example data

The hub's view of the Now panel's train card: on weekday mornings, from 06:00 until the 07:16 has left, a card at the top of Home. The departure sits in a labelled box so it does not read as a clock; the status is the large line, then when and from which platform it leaves, the stops with their times, and a ring counting down.

When it shows

  • Weekdays from 06:00 until the train has left (its actual departure, so a late train stays until it goes). The rest of the day the card is gone and Home looks as today.
  • Above the other cards, full width: it is the one thing on Home with a deadline.
  • Same data as the Now panel and the Telegram warnings, from /api/train.

States

  • On time: green status, a green ring.
  • Late: the planned time struck through beside the new one, every stop's time in amber, the status says by how much.
  • Platform change: the new platform in an amber chip with the old one struck through; the status stays as it is.
  • Disruption on the route: an amber note with NS's own text, under the stops.
  • Cancelled: red, the stops dimmed and struck through, and the next direct train in its own box; the ring counts down to that one.

Also

  • Stale: the usual "last known" note when the NS API does not answer.
  • Telegram, as decided: only problems (late by 3 min or more, cancelled, platform change, a new disruption), skipping quiet hours.
