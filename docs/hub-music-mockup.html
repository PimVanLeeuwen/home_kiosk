Music example data

Spotify in the hub: a Music page with Now Playing (the cover with its record spinning out behind it, lit in the album's colour), the controls, your Spotify devices and four favourite playlists; and on Home a Now Playing card while something plays.

Now Playing

  • The cover with a record sliding out behind it that spins while it plays (its label is the cover), on a blurred background in the album's colours; the progress line and the play button glow in its main colour, taken from the cover by the hub.
  • Title, artist (or the show for a podcast), progress, previous, play or pause, next, volume, and the device it plays on as a chip.
  • Paused: the record slides back, the equaliser stops. Nothing playing: what played last and the favourites to start from.

Devices and playlists

  • Your Spotify Connect devices (the phone, the Mac, an Apple TV with the Spotify app open), the active one lit; tap another to move playback there.
  • HomePods are not Spotify Connect devices: music sent to them over AirPlay shows as playing on the phone or Mac it comes from, and the hub cannot start music on a HomePod itself.
  • Four favourite playlists, chosen and ordered in Settings → Music; tap one to start it on the active device (or the last used one).
  • Covers through the hub, as everywhere: the browser never loads from Spotify.

Home and freshness

  • On Home a Now Playing card only while something plays or is paused (gone two minutes after a pause, as on the panel).
  • The hub asks Spotify only while a page, the card or a panel is looking, at most every 2 s; the progress line moves on its own in between. Nothing is kept about what you played.
  • Connect once in Settings → Music (on the ts.net address); a write to Spotify, like the lights, is same-origin only.
