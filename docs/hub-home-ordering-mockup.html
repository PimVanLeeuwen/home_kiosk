Home, ordered by what matters now example data

The most important thing right now gets the hero: the train in the morning, the music in the evening, a problem in red. Then the live cards, then the rest in an order for the part of the day. Cards that only say "all good" fold into the status strip; a chip opens its card. "Why here" shows each card's reason.

How the order is made

  • Hero: the first of: a problem (a failed backup, a failing disk, a service down, an outside incident), the train before it leaves, music playing, an event now. Its own light follows its state: green for an on-time train, the cover's colours for music, red for a problem. Nothing to show means no hero, and the cards move up.
  • Live: the other live cards next: laundry running or done, an event within the hour, an Apple device playing. They get a faint violet (or green when done) light in their corner.
  • Part of the day: the rest in the order below. Cards with nothing to show stay hidden, as today.
  • Folded: NAS, Services, Backups and Outside services live in the status strip while all is well, each chip with a small meter (storage, services running, nights backed up, components clear). A chip opens its card below the strip, one at a time.

Look and motion

  • The sky: two soft lights at the top for the part of the day (amber and rose at dawn, violet in the day, indigo at dusk, almost none at night) and a fine grain, so the dark has depth without colour washing over the cards.
  • Two stacks instead of rows: each card has its column and the order runs within it, so a short card never leaves a gap. On the phone one column, by rank.
  • One staggered reveal when the page opens. When the order changes the cards slide to their place in about 300 ms, never while the pointer is over a card or a chip is open. Nothing loops; no motion with reduced motion.
