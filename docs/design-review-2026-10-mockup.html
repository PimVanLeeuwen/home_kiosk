01

New screens, made for the desk

Your decision of 2026-10-10 reads "no ambient mode, the panels always show their screens". These are screens in the rotation, not a mode, and every one still tells you something: the time, the light, the sky, the day. You pick which panel turns through which.

The hub paints, the panel showsThe rich layer (light, glow, grain, art) is a 720 × 720 picture the hub paints with Pillow, as it already does for headline pictures.
The panel adds what is liveThe time, the words and one or two moving shapes go on top in LVGL, so the screen stays current without repainting the picture.
Within the ESP32-P4A JPEG through the hardware decoder, a few labels, at most a handful of moving objects; nothing that stalls LVGL.

ALiving sky

New · Now · the clock's calm face

A slowly flowing gradient in the colours of the sky right now: peach and gold at golden hour, clear blue at noon, indigo after dark, greyed when it rains. The time sits in it, light and large, with what the light does next.

Sunrise, sunset and the weather the hub already reads; the hub paints the gradient every few minutes.

BNight sky

New · Now · after dark, on a clear night

The sky as it is: the moon in its phase, where it stands, the stars and a constellation that is up, the glow of the town at the horizon, and now and then a shooting star.

The moon's phase and position, computed in the hub; clear or cloudy from the weather.

COrbit clock

New · any panel

The time as a small solar system: the hour, the minute and the second as bodies on their orbits round a warm sun, the second leaving a fading trail. Calm, readable from across the room once you know it.

Nothing; three moving objects on a painted background.

DDaylight ring

New · Now or House

The whole day as a 24-hour ring: night, twilight and daylight in their colours, the sun where it is now, and how much light today has against yesterday. Most striking around the solstices.

Sunrise, sunset and twilight times, computed in the hub.

EPhoto of the day

New · Home or House

Immich's "on this day" memory, drifting slowly, with where and when on a frosted label. A favourite album when the day has none.

An Immich key that may read memories and photos; the hub cuts a 720 px JPEG.

FArt of the day

New · any panel

Every morning a new generative piece, painted by the hub from the date: thousands of lines following a flow field, in a palette of the season. Numbered, never the same twice.

Nothing outside the hub; a few seconds of Pillow at 05:00.

GWord clock

New · any panel

The time in words on a grid of letters, the lit ones glowing, the corner dots for the minutes between. Every five minutes the changing words cross-fade.

Nothing.

HThe year

New · House or Now

The year as a ring of 365 days, the months around it, the days gone in the panels' violet and today glowing at its place. In the middle, how far along it is.

Nothing.

IRain radar

New · Now · only when rain comes

The radar loop around home for the next two hours, with the rain to come as a strip of bars under it. It only joins the rotation when rain is expected.

Buienradar's public radar images, fetched by the hub.

JYour week

New · Now

Seven days: each day's warmth from low to high, the line of the highs across them, the sky and the rain, and dots for days with events.

The forecast for 7 days instead of 2, the calendar for the week.

KCountdown

New · Home or Now

Something to look forward to: the days to a trip or a birthday, large, with each day left as a dot. Set from a calendar event marked as a countdown.

The calendar; a tag or a word in the event's title.

02

The screens you have, finished

Three screens with room to spare, and one bit of motion for every screen.

LTrain: the route as a line

Now · fills the empty band

The band between the countdown and the stops becomes the route as a line. Once the train has left, it moves along it as a glowing dot, placed from its planned and actual times.

MMusic: a wall of covers

Home · fills the empty middle

Eight covers over the colour of what plays: your favourites, then what played lately, each a tap to play. The covers are already stored for Now Playing.

NArrival motion

Every panel · each time a screen comes round

A screen arrives: the ring sweeps to its value, the number counts up, the small charts draw in, about 700 ms, then all is still.

The same information, given a signature visual each: light where something is on, motion where something flows, depth where things sit. The text, the numbers and the layout of what you read stay where they are; the rich layer is the hub's picture, the moving part one or two LVGL objects.
