Desk panels Example data

Three 4-inch touch panels on one stand: information, home and music, and a desk piece in their own right. Swipe a panel, tap the dots, tap a room to switch it, hold a room for its brightness.

Now

  • Clock with the next two hours of Buienradar rain as a ring around it, starting at now
  • Your train: the 07:16 departure to Rotterdam Centraal, on weekday mornings only (tap Preview for delayed and cancelled)
  • Today: the next event with a countdown ring, the day as a ribbon with a now line, and what comes later
  • Your day: the sun's arc over a sky band from night through dawn, day and dusk, the temperature curve and the chance of rain
  • Headlines: a full-bleed lead story, two more with thumbnails; tap one to save it to Readeck (the bookmark fills)
  • Night: a dim clock only

Home

  • The living room only: the 3 kitchen spots and the 2 lamps, each glowing in the white they have now
  • Tap to switch, hold and drag for brightness; warm to cool white for the kitchen spots
  • Air: temperature, humidity, CO₂ as a ring with Fresh, Getting stuffy or Open a window, PM2.5, and CO₂ over the last 8 hours; the Christmas plugs only in the season
  • Laundry (only while a machine runs or is done): the Hisense washer and dryer as portholes with water turning while they run, a ring counting down and when they are ready
  • Apple devices: a small map of the house, each Apple TV and HomePod where it stands, playing ones rippling in the colour of what they play, the Thread mesh and the air sensor
  • Music: Now Playing first while music plays; otherwise a card with favourite playlists to start from the desk

House

  • My sites: one ring per site, 30 day segments each, incidents lit amber or red; Services (GitHub, Cloudflare, Docker Hub, Tailscale, Hetzner, TransIP, Proton, with their logos): a constellation around a check when all is well, an incident card with its timeline when not (tap the status to preview)
  • NAS as three rings with the storage runway; backups as the last 14 nights; alerts with Mute 1h, or All quiet when nothing is open (tap the status to preview)
  • Money: the portfolio over an edge-to-edge chart with a day, week, month or year switch; top movers as a heat map sized by holding, with the indices
  • Code: 16 weeks of activity, CI state and pull requests. Media: what is downloading with progress and time left, and missing episodes from Sonarr
  • Night: screen off, unless an alert needs you

An error alert takes over all three panels until dismissed. Which cards each panel shows, their order and timing, and the night hours are set in the hub under Settings → Desk panels. Album art here is generated colour, not real covers. All figures are made up.
