Desk panels Example data

Three 4-inch touch panels on one stand: information, home and music, and a desk piece in their own right. Swipe a panel, tap the dots, tap a room to switch it, hold a room for its brightness.

Now

  • Clock, weather and Buienradar rain, on a sky that follows the time of day
  • Your train: the 07:16 departure to Rotterdam Centraal, on weekday mornings only (tap Preview for delayed and cancelled)
  • Today: events from the chosen Apple calendars, the next one up
  • Your day: the sun's arc, the temperature curve and the chance of rain, with a one-line summary
  • Headlines: a lead story with its image, then two more; tap one to save it to Readeck
  • Night: a dim clock only

Home

  • The living room only: the 3 kitchen spots and the 2 lamps, each glowing in the white they have now
  • Tap to switch, hold and drag for brightness; warm to cool white for the kitchen spots
  • Scenes (Relax, Read, Concentrate, Dimmed) and All off; the Christmas plugs only in the season
  • Music: Now Playing first while music plays; otherwise a card with favourite playlists to start from the desk

House

  • My sites: one ring per site, 30 day segments each, incidents lit amber or red; Services (GitHub, Cloudflare, Docker Hub, Tailscale, Hetzner, Proton): a constellation around a check when all is well, an incident card with its timeline when not (tap the status to preview)
  • NAS as three rings with the storage runway; backups as the last 14 nights; alerts with Mute 1h, or All quiet when nothing is open (tap the status to preview)
  • Money: portfolio with a day, week, month or year switch, then top movers as diverging bars
  • Code: 16 weeks of activity, CI state and pull requests. Jellyfin: continue watching, recently added, downloads
  • Night: screen off, unless an alert needs you

An error alert takes over all three panels until dismissed. Which cards each panel shows, their order and timing, and the night hours are set in the hub under Settings → Desk panels. Album art here is generated colour, not real covers. All figures are made up.
