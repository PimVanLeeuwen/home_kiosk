Home, restyled example data

The hub's Home page in the desk panels' language, calmer: rings, glows and halos only where they carry meaning (state, progress, something that needs you). Same cards, same data the API already has.

What changes, per card

  • Page: one soft glow at the top that follows the time of day (violet by day, deeper at night); cards get a faint top sheen. No ambient light behind individual cards.
  • Status strip: glowing dots; a chip that needs a look turns amber with a slow halo, the only motion on a calm page.
  • Portfolio: the 30-day chart bleeds to the card edges with a glowing line, a gradient and the period's high; holdings with ticker chips and tinted day changes.
  • NAS: an arc gauge for the fullest pool (ticks, a red mark at the alert threshold, the runway below), glass tiles with 24-hour sparklines for CPU and memory, a glowing dot per disk and the UPS.
  • Backups: a verified seal with last night's time, duration and size, the restore check, the route to Hetzner with a slow light, and 30 nights as bars (a slow night amber, last night bright).

 

  • News: the newest story leads in larger type; group chips in their own colour; unread dots glow. (No thumbnails: the article list has no images today.)
  • Services: unchanged apart from glowing status dots.
  • Weather: the temperature inside a ring of the next two hours of rain (as on the Now panel), and the sun's day as an arc with where the sun is now.
  • Outside services: as built in step 3.
  • Motion: only the amber halo, the backup light and the incident halo; all of it stops with reduced motion.
