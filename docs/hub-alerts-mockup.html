Alerts, restyled example data

The Alerts page in the language of the other restyled pages: the open alerts lead (or "All quiet" with slow ripples and the last seven days), the messages as a timeline coloured by severity, the rules with glowing switches, and quiet hours on a 24-hour ring. Same data as today.

What changes

  • Header: as today, plus an All quiet / N open pill.
  • Open alerts (on a phone first, then the messages, then the settings): with nothing open, a check with slow ripples, "All quiet", how long nothing has needed you, and a dot per day of the last week (amber or red on days that had an alert). Each open alert is a tinted card with a pulsing icon, the severity, a big title, its detail and how long it has been open. Worked out from the incident list the hub already keeps.
  • Messages: the compose bar in glass; the filters as pills; every message on a timeline with a dot in its severity's colour, the time, a source chip and the severity, the title and body, and how it was delivered (sent, held for the digest, or failed in red).
  • Hub rules: a glowing switch per rule, its numbers in small glass fields inside the sentence.
  • Delivery: quiet hours as a 24-hour ring with the quiet span lit and a mark for now; the chat per category as before.
  • Bot commands: the commands as small mono chips.
  • Not here: the panel's "Mute 1h" button; the hub has no mute yet, so it would be a new feature.
  • Motion: the ripples and the pulsing icon only; both stop with reduced motion.
