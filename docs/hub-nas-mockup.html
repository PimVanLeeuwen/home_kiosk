NAS, restyled example data

The NAS page in the same language as the restyled Home: instruments with glowing 24-hour lines, a dial per pool, disks as drive bays, the UPS as a battery ring, and the exposure audit behind a shield. Same data as today.

What changes

  • Header: host and uptime as today, plus a Healthy / Needs a look pill (the same rule as the Home card).
  • CPU, Memory, CPU temperature, Network: the figure large with its 24-hour peak, and the day as a glowing area running edge to edge, the peak marked with a dot. Temperature turns amber from 70 °C; network shows download as the area and upload as a thin line.
  • Storage: an arc gauge per pool (the same as on Home), used and free below, the runway in amber under 6 months, fullest first; one thin bar for all pools together.
  • Disks: each disk as a drive bay: an LED in its SMART colour (pulsing when it is not healthy), the name, a temperature bar from cool blue to red, the figure and the SMART word. Containers below with a link to Docker.
  • UPS: the battery as a ring in its state's colour with the charge inside, the state, load and model beside it; power cuts as a timeline (red when services stopped), and the note about 15%.
  • Exposure: a shield seal with the verdict, every public hostname as a glass row with a Tunnel or DNS chip, its target and answer (an unexpected one tinted red), and the port scan as its own line with the number of ports checked.
  • Motion: only the pulse on a disk, UPS or host that needs a look; it stops with reduced motion.
