Today on Home example data

The Now panel's calendar card in the hub: the next event large with a ring counting down to it in its calendar's colour, the day from 07:00 to 23:00 as a ribbon of coloured blocks with a glowing now line, then the rest of the day. Today only, full titles, read-only; which calendars show is set in Settings.

The card

  • Where: first in Home's grid, before Portfolio. A day without events leaves it out.
  • Next: its calendar's name in its colour, the title in full, the time; the ring empties over the last hour before it starts. During an event: "Now", until when, and the ring counts down to its end.
  • All-day events (birthdays, holidays) as chips under the next event.
  • Ribbon: 07:00 to 23:00, each event a block in its calendar's colour, past ones dimmed, a glowing line at now.
  • The rest of the day below, past ones struck through and dimmed; "Nothing more today" once the last one has ended.

Source and settings

  • iCloud over CalDAV with an app-specific password (NAS-only .env), read every 5 minutes; repeating events unfolded for today.
  • Settings, Calendars: every iCloud calendar with its own colour and a switch; off means it never shows here or on the panel. Saved in the hub's settings.
  • Stale and error states as everywhere ("Calendar not available right now").
