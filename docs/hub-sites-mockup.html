My sites on Services example data

A new section on the Services page, above Outside services: the House panel's My sites card in the hub. One ring per site, 30 day segments going round from 30 days ago (at the top) to today; a good day quiet green, a blip amber, an outage red with a glow; the sites up in the middle; beside it the sites in their Kuma groups with their 30-day uptime, shown only below 100%.

The section

  • Rings: one per site, outermost first in the list's order; 30 segments from 30 days ago (at the top, a small tick) round to today. A day counts as an outage when a site was down for more than 5 minutes, a blip for anything shorter; a site down right now pulses its last segment.
  • Middle: the sites up now, "7/7 up"; amber or red with the ring when one is not.
  • List: Kuma's groups (Personal, Hubble) and its short names; each with its colour key and its 30-day uptime, shown only below 100%; a site down now says since when.
  • Source: Uptime Kuma's "sites" status page (public, no key), read every minute. The hub keeps its own day record per site in SQLite, so the 30 days do not depend on how much history Kuma returns; the rings fill up from the day it starts.
  • Telegram: unchanged, Kuma already sends a message through the hub when a site goes down.
  • Home: the status strip counts a site that is down; no card of its own.
